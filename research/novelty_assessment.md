# Novelty assessment

Classification: **Incremental**.

The proposed method is a specific two-step fourth-order method of the
Newton-correction type. The area is heavily populated; we cannot establish
that (1) is absent from the literature. The genuinely verifiable parts are:
the symbolic verification of the order, the open implementation, and the
reproducible experiments. We therefore do not claim first discovery.

Uncertainty: high. The formula may well have appeared under another name.
