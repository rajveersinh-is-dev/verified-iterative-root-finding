# Journal selection

## Recommended journal
IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis (IMAJNA), Oxford University Press (hybrid).

## Why
- Scope: original contributions to all fields of numerical analysis,
  including theory, development, and use of practical algorithms. Fits.
- Format: standard research article; LaTeX source required after acceptance.

## Fee policy (verified 2026-10-18)
- Route: standard (subscription) publication. No mandatory author
  publication fee. Optional gold open access available with an APC.
- Additional charges: colour in print £350 per figure (online colour free).
  No page charges stated.
- Source: https://academic.oup.com/imajna/pages/General_Instructions
  ("Open Access Option for Authors": optional OA with charge; standard
  licence available without charge).
- APC price list: https://academic.oup.com/pages/open-research/open-access/charges-licences-and-self-archiving

## Preprint / public repository policy
- Preprint policy: author's original version (preprint) may be posted
  anywhere at any time; does not prevent submission.
- Accepted manuscript: custom embargo policy (IMA journals are "Custom").
  Accepted MS may be posted to personal webpage immediately; institutional/
  non-commercial repositories only after embargo. Commercial repositories
  (including GitHub, unless licensed) require care. We therefore post only
  the preprint-level manuscript and code in the public repo, and document
  the distinction. Data/code sharing is encouraged by the journal.
- Source: https://academic.oup.com/imajna/pages/General_Instructions
  (Preprint Policy, Author Self-Archiving/Public Access Policy).

## Alternatives considered
1. Information and Inference: A Journal of the IMA (OUP) — scope more
   ML/inference; 12-month embargo; less fit.
2. The Computer Journal (OUP) — more software/engineering; possible but
   less fit for a numerical-analysis note.

## Ambiguities
- Exact embargo length for IMAJNA accepted manuscripts is "Custom"; confirm
  with the editorial office before posting an accepted manuscript.
